Our Purpose

A World Without Packaging Waste

We believe packaging should protect your products, not damage global ecosystems. Our mission is to make the transition to circular logistics seamless, affordable, and highly measurable.

  • Zero-Waste Lifecycle

    100% of our products compost back into healthy soils or enter standard recycling loops.

  • Smart Bio-Materials

    Proprietary plant-based starches engineered to resist moisture and heavy transit loads.

  • Carbon Reductions

    Scope 3 carbon offsets tracked, verified, and reported live for every SKU you ship.

  • Ethical Supply Chain

    Guaranteed living wages, employee equity, and strict safety audits across all processing nodes.

Our Journey

Growing Towards a Sustainable Future

Since our founding, we have pushed the boundaries of supply chain innovation to deliver scalable circularity.

2018

Company Founded

VerdantPack was established in Austin, Texas by three supply chain engineers committed to developing zero-waste bulk shipping alternatives.

2019

First Sustainable Product

Launched our signature plant-starch compostable shipping mailers, passing rigorous ASTM D6400 industrial standards.

2021

Nationwide Expansion

Opened regional warehousing nodes in California and New Jersey, cutting average transit distances by 32% for domestic shipping.

2023

Carbon Neutral Operations

Certified Carbon Neutral by ClimatePartner after fully auditing and offsetting Scope 1, 2, and 3 footprint operations.

2025

Serving Thousands of Brands

Over 18,000 active e-commerce partners globally, replacing over 100 million single-use plastic boxes and envelopes.
