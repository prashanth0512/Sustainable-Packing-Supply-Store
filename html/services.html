Common Questions About Our Packaging

Everything you need to know about our certifications, volume limits, and carbon neutrality.

We dispatch standard in-stock catalog orders within 24-48 hours. Domestic transit times range between 2 to 5 business days, dispatched from our nearest regional micro-fulfillment center to minimize shipping mileage and Scope 3 greenhouse gas footprints.

Yes. Wholesale pricing scales automatically. Discount brackets begin at 1,000 units, with increased price breaks at 5,000, 10,000, and 25,000 units. Contract packaging clients enjoy automated monthly replenishment pipelines.

Our compostable bags are made from a proprietary blend of PLA (polylactic acid, harvested from corn/sugarcane starches) and PBAT (a biodegradable co-polymer). They contain zero petroleum residues and break down cleanly leaving no microplastic residues.

By operating our own raw processing nodes and localizing production, we eliminate the 'green premium'. In bulk capacities, VerdantPack achieves cost parity with traditional petroleum plastics, enabling scalable sustainability without budget strain.

Absolutely. We offer complete custom branding options using water-based and soy-based non-toxic inks. These organic pigments decompose safely along with the packaging and do not impact compostability or cardboard recyclability.

Our carbon-neutral shipping covers 100% of transport-related Scope 3 emissions. We calculate carbon output per SKU and freight distance, offsetting the footprint by investing in verified reforestation and clean wind/solar projects globally.

Our packaging catalog holds certifications from global environmental bodies, including BPI Certified Compostable, FSC Chain of Custody (for all wood fibers), OK Compost Home, and ISO 14001 certification for quality and environmental control.

We pride ourselves on manufacturing reliability. If any batch fails compression, moisture-resistance, or adhesive shear tests, we offer full replacements or refunds within 30 days of shipment delivery.
